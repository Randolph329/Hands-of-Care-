Housing Assistance

Tip: Start with the top agencies below. If you’re not sure where to begin, contact Hands of Care and we’ll guide you.

Washington, DC
  • DC Housing Authority (DCHA) — Public housing & Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8).
  • DC Department of Human Services (DHS) — Emergency rental assistance & housing support.
  • Housing Counseling Services — Housing counseling, eviction help, foreclosure prevention.
Maryland
  • Maryland DHCD — State housing programs & rental assistance.
  • Maryland Access Point — Senior-focused resource navigation & referrals.
  • Maryland Legal Aid — Tenant rights & housing legal support.
Virginia
  • Virginia Housing — Affordable housing & rental programs.
  • VA DHCD — Housing & community development programs.
  • Legal Aid Justice Center — Tenant support & housing justice.
Need help navigating? Visit the Contact page and send a message. Hands of Care can help you find the right next step.
