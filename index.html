About Hands of Care

Hands of Care is a senior support platform focused on helping individuals maintain independence while receiving trusted, background-checked assistance.

Need Help With applications?

Many senior programs require applications, documents, and waiting lists. If you're unsure where to start, Hands of Care can help guide you step-by-step.

Our resource directory is free to use. If you need personal help navigating applications, gathering documents, or choosing the right programs, Hands of Care can assist you.
