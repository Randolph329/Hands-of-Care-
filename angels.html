Community Angels

Community Angels are trusted people who step up to help seniors, families, and neighbors get the support they need.

Hands of Care is building a network of people who can help with rides, home support, food help, wellness checks, basic home repairs, yard help, and community rebuilding.

Ways Community Angels Can Help

  • Help seniors get to appointments
  • Assist with food, errands, and daily needs
  • Support home repair and cleanup requests
  • Check on elderly neighbors
  • Connect people to resources
  • Create jobs and opportunities in the community

Who Can Become a Community Angel?

People with a heart to serve. Helpers, drivers, caregivers, handymen, cleaners, cooks, organizers, mentors, and responsible community members.

Our Goal

To bring people back together, help our elders live longer and stronger, rebuild homes, create local jobs, and build a community support system that starts in the DMV and grows worldwide.
