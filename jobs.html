Community Jobs & Opportunities

Hands of Care believes strong communities create strong futures. We connect people with opportunities to help seniors, families, and neighborhoods while earning income and building experience.

Senior Companion

Visit seniors, provide companionship, assist with errands, and help reduce loneliness.

Transportation Helper

Help seniors get to doctor appointments, grocery stores, pharmacies, and community events.

Home Repair Assistant

Assist with small repairs, painting, yard work, cleaning, and safety improvements.

Community Cleanup Team

Work with local residents to clean streets, remove litter, improve neighborhoods, and create safer communities.

Technology Helper

Help seniors use smartphones, computers, email, video calls, and online services.

Future Opportunities

As Hands of Care grows, additional paid positions will become available throughout DC, Maryland, Virginia, and beyond.
